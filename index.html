Our Story

Founded in 2024, FranchiseOne set out with a bold mission: to transform the franchise industry in Indonesia and beyond. We recognized a significant gap in the market, a lack of comprehensive, end-to-end solutions for franchise businesses aiming to scale effectively.

Leveraging our deep expertise in the FMCG, commodity, and F&B sectors, we have crafted a uniquely integrated approach that fuses strategic consulting, operational excellence, and cutting-edge technology solutions.

Today, FranchiseOne is proud to empower emerging local brands and established global franchises as they successfully navigate the complexities of the Indonesian market, fostering sustainable growth and long-term success.
